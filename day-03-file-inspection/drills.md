last 15 lines, count its lines, identify its file type, and inspect its full metadata with stat._

1. cat sample.txt
2. less sample.txt
3. head -n 15 sample.txt && tail -n 15 sample.txt
4. wc -l sample.txt
5. file sample.txt
6. stat sample.txt
